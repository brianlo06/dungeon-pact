# Debt of Thunder — Arcanist

**Idea.** The Arcanist does not call a storm, it borrows one: the tome tears
itself into the sky, a promissory seal is written in the clouds, every enemy is
drowned in the rain that follows, and the debt is called in at once — one bolt
through the seal, and Chain Shock running down the soaked line.

**Gameplay** (`ultimate-defs/arcanist.ts`): every enemy Wet; 26 lightning on the
target, which sets off Chain Shock on the rest; the rest stay Wet for the party.
The film shows the arcs because they are the reaction the party keeps using.

**Rhythm.** identity (the book) → stakes (the whole line, drenched) →
anticipation (the rain *stops*; silence under the brim) → escalation (hand to
the seal) → impact (black frame, bolt, chain) → release (steam on the real board).

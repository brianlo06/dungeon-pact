# The Hundredth Mark — Ranger

**Idea.** The Ranger sees every shot it could take — a hundred possible lines,
each leaving a quarry sign on the enemy it would hit — and then lets
ninety-nine of them go and looses the one. "One mark. A hundred
possibilities": the marks on the whole line are the possibilities left for the
party; one is finished.

**Gameplay** (`ultimate-defs/ranger.ts`, unchanged): every enemy Marked; 24
precision through armour on the target, which cashes its Mark (EXPLOIT, +20);
the rest stay Marked for the party's precision hits; +2 Focus. The film shows
a sign on every real enemy because that *is* the effect, and the one sign that
breaks is the Exploit.

**Space.** The wood the Ranger learned in: first light, ground fog, birch
trunks scored with a kid's tally marks, which light as it counts. The only
bright space among the films — figures are ink against fog. Palette: fog
white-green, ink, one lime (`#c7f08a`) for lines and signs, and the red of the
drawn arrow's fletching.

**Rhythm, varied.** identity (the draw to the cheek) → stakes and escalation
(a hundred lines across the whole line; a sign on each, the target last;
title) → decision (the face: seeing all of it, then one; ninety-nine lines go
out) → the loose, hidden in a white frame → **quiet** (the arrow in flight:
silence, slowed time, the fog, the ghosts of the other lines) → impact (the
sign cashed in) → release (the marks left on the real board). The quietest
shot sits directly before the loudest.

**Room for the paths.** The base film owns the mark and the choice. The
Sharpshooter can own distance and armour (the flat, fast shot), the Pathfinder
the ground and the snare, the Gloomstalker the dark and the unseen shot — none
of which this film uses.

**Framing.** Large and boss targets are pulled back in the flight and the
impact so the whole figure and its sign stay in frame; the boss's sign and the
screen reticle follow the target's real chest, wherever its size puts it. In
the sight shot a boss stands further into the fog.

**Voice.** "Hold still. I'm counting." · "…a hundred." · "The rest are yours!"
